FAQs

Good to know

Where are you?

43 Palmerston Road, Boscombe, Bournemouth BH1 4HW. Open in Google Maps.

What are your opening hours?

Please check our Facebook page or call 07816 151131 for today’s opening times.

Do you make birthday and celebration cakes?

Yes. Send us an order enquiry with the date, size and your ideas, and we’ll get back to you. The more notice, the better.

Can I eat in?

Yes. Grab a coffee and something sweet and stay a while, or take it away.

Do you cater for allergies?

Please ask a member of the team about allergens before you order. Our kitchen handles flour, nuts, eggs and dairy.

Ordering a cake or a big batch?

Send us a message with the date and what you’d like, and we’ll get back to you.
