Our story

Good baking for Boscombe

Baked Boscombe is a small, independent bakery and café on Palmerston Road, in the heart of Boscombe.

We believe everyone deserves a really good bake: bread with a proper crust, pastries that flake, and cakes that make you smile. Everything is made with care, and the counter changes daily.

Whether you’re grabbing a coffee on the way to work, meeting a friend for cake or ordering something special for a birthday, we’d love to see you.
